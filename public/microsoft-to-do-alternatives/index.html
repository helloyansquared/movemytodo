Microsoft To Do alternatives, compared by how your tasks get there

Every to-do app can replace Microsoft To Do; the hard part is bringing years of lists with you. Todoist and TickTick have free To Do importers and Superlist can sync with To Do, while Google Tasks, Apple Reminders and Things 3 have no importer, which is the gap MoveMyTodo fills. Work and school accounts are pointed to Planner, but existing tasks aren't copied into it.

Three questions to decide

  1. Which devices do you use? Only Apple devices: Reminders or Things. Android or Windows too: Google Tasks, Todoist or TickTick.
  2. Do you share lists? Shared lists don't move as shared with any method; you re-share them in the new app. Check that it supports sharing the way your family or team needs before you move.
  3. Free or paid? Google Tasks and Reminders are free. Todoist and TickTick have free plans with limits. Things is a one-time purchase per device.

Whichever you choose

Download a free backup first, so you keep a copy that doesn't depend on Microsoft or the new app. Then move, and keep To Do for a week or two until you're sure everything arrived.

Questions

What's the best Microsoft To Do alternative?

It depends on what you need and how much moving you want to do. Todoist and TickTick have their own free importers; Superlist keeps a live sync with To Do; Google Tasks, Apple Reminders and Things 3 are simpler or Apple-only apps without an importer, which is where MoveMyTodo helps. Work and school accounts are pointed to Planner, but tasks aren't copied into it.

Will my To Do tasks move to Planner or another app automatically?

No. Microsoft says existing To Do tasks and lists will not migrate to Planner, and personal accounts can't use Planner at all. You have to export or move them yourself before June 30, 2027.

What about Todoist or TickTick?

Both have their own free Microsoft To Do importers, so you don't need MoveMyTodo to get there. Todoist's importer (launched November 21, 2026) copies the lists you choose with due dates, notes and steps; TickTick's is in its web app under Settings → Integrations & Import. MoveMyTodo is for the apps without an importer: Google Tasks, Apple Reminders and Things 3. Either way, download the free backup first so you keep a copy you control.

What if I wait until after June 30, 2027?

The apps will be gone, and Microsoft hasn't promised how long the export or API will keep working after that. Back up now; it takes a minute and costs nothing.
