How to move Microsoft To Do to Google Tasks

Google Tasks has no import feature, so the only ways across are retyping or a tool that writes through Google's Tasks API. With MoveMyTodo you sign in to Microsoft (read-only), pick your lists, then connect Google Tasks: each To Do list becomes a Google Tasks list, steps become subtasks, and notes, due dates and completed tasks come along.

Steps

  1. On MoveMyTodo, click Sign in with Microsoft and use the account you use for To Do. Microsoft asks you to allow read access to your tasks.
  2. Check the numbers (lists, tasks, steps) and untick any lists you don't want. Download the free CSV backup while you're there.
  3. In Move to your new app, choose Google Tasks and click Connect Google Tasks & move.
  4. Pick your Google account and allow access to Google Tasks. While Google's review of MoveMyTodo isn't finished, Google first shows an extra screen saying “Google hasn't verified this app”; choose Advanced there to continue. The lists appear in Google Tasks as they're written; a big account takes a few minutes.
  5. Open tasks.google.com, Gmail's side panel or the Google Tasks app to check them.

What carries over

Microsoft To Do → Google Tasks
ListsA new Google Tasks list for each To Do list. If you already have a list with the same name, the new one is called “… (from To Do)”.
StepsBecome subtasks (Google Tasks allows one level), ticked if they were done.
Notes and linksCopied into the task's details. Google allows about 8,000 characters per note.
Due datesCopied. Google Tasks stores a date, not a time.
Reminders and repeatsWritten into the notes (“Reminder: 2026-12-03 09:00”, “Repeats: Every month on day 1”). Google's Tasks API can't set them, so add them again for the tasks that need them.
Completed tasksOptional. Moved as completed, under “Completed” in each list.
OrderKept: open tasks first, in To Do's order.

Without a tool

For a few tasks, retyping is fine. For more, there's no file you can upload to Google Tasks: it has no import menu, and Google Calendar's import creates events, not tasks. Automation services such as Zapier only copy new tasks as they're created, not the ones you already have.

Questions

Can I move Microsoft To Do to Google Tasks?

Yes. MoveMyTodo signs in to Google Tasks with your permission and creates a list for each To Do list, with steps as subtasks, notes, due dates and completed tasks. Google Tasks has no import button of its own. Until Google finishes reviewing MoveMyTodo, Google shows an extra “Google hasn't verified this app” screen first; choose Advanced there to continue.

What happens to repeating tasks and reminders?

Due dates move everywhere. Google Tasks, Apple Reminders and Things can't receive a repeat rule through an import, so MoveMyTodo writes it into the task's notes, for example “Repeats: Every month on day 1”, and you set it again in the new app.

Is it safe to sign in? What can MoveMyTodo see?

MoveMyTodo asks Microsoft for read-only access to your tasks, so it cannot change or delete anything in To Do. Your tasks are read straight into your browser tab and are never sent to or stored on our servers. Close the tab and they're gone.

Is MoveMyTodo free?

Scanning, the full backup (CSV, Markdown, JSON, printable) and Todoist files are free with no limits. Moving to Apple Reminders or Things is free for up to 25 tasks from one list; MoveMyTodo Pro moves everything, including to Google Tasks, for $6.99 once. (Google Tasks is Pro-only while Google verifies MoveMyTodo, because Google caps unverified apps at 100 users.)
