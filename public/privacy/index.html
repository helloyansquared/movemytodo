Privacy policy

MoveMyTodo reads your Microsoft To Do tasks inside your own browser tab, and only when you ask it to. Your tasks are never sent to, stored on or seen by our servers.

Effective: November 26, 2026 · Last updated: November 26, 2026

What you allow, and why

MicrosoftTasks.Read (read your tasks) plus basic sign-in (your name and email, to show which account you used). Read-only: MoveMyTodo can't change or delete anything in To Do.
GoogleGoogle Tasks (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks), asked for only when you click “Connect Google Tasks & move”. It's the narrowest Google permission that can create tasks; the read-only one can't. MoveMyTodo asks for no other Google data: not Gmail, Drive, Calendar or Contacts.

Where your data goes

Sign-in happens on Microsoft's and Google's own pages. The access they grant is kept only in the memory of your browser tab, never saved to disk or sent to us, and ends when you close the tab (Microsoft and Google tokens also expire within about an hour). Your tasks travel directly between your browser and Microsoft's and Google's servers. Files you download (backups, the Apple Reminders script, Todoist files) are made in your browser.

Google user data

What we access. With the Google Tasks permission, MoveMyTodo reads the names of your existing task lists (so a new list never overwrites one with the same name) and creates new task lists and tasks: title, notes, due date, completed status and subtasks, copied from the Microsoft To Do lists you chose. It doesn't read the contents of your existing Google tasks, and it doesn't change or delete anything in Google Tasks.

How we use it. Only to carry out the move you start by clicking the button, and only while this browser tab is open. Nothing runs in the background or later.

Storage and retention. None. Google's access token and your task data stay in your browser tab's memory and are discarded when you close or reload it. MoveMyTodo has no database, and no Google user data ever reaches our servers or logs.

Sharing. We don't share, transfer, sell or disclose Google user data to anyone. Your tasks travel directly between your browser and Google over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

Limited Use. MoveMyTodo's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We don't use Google user data for advertising, and we don't use it to develop, improve or train AI or machine-learning models. No human at YanSquared can read it.

License keys and payments

If you buy MoveMyTodo Pro, payment is handled by Dodo Payments (merchant of record), which processes your card, sales tax and receipt under its own privacy policy. When you activate Pro, your license key is sent to our small checking service (a Cloudflare Pages Function), which asks Dodo Payments whether it's valid. The key and an activation ID are saved in your browser's local storage so Pro stays unlocked. The checking service doesn't keep the key and never receives your tasks.

Hosting, cookies and analytics

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any host, Cloudflare processes standard request data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site. MoveMyTodo uses no cookies, ads or third-party analytics. To learn which sites and AI assistants send visitors, the first page you open sends one anonymous note to a database we keep with our host, Cloudflare: the name of the site you came from (such as “google” or “chatgpt”, never the full address), the page and your country. While you use the tool, it also counts which steps were reached, once per visit (for example signed in, downloaded a backup, moved a list, opened checkout, unlocked Pro), with a rough size band such as “up to 100 tasks”, never a task, list name or anything you wrote. After a backup or move, a small optional card asks how you found the site; if you answer, your choice and anything you type in the optional box are stored the same way. Cloudflare works out the country from the connection; your IP address is used only briefly, in memory, to stop spam and is never stored, and these notes are deleted after 180 days. None of this includes your tasks, account, IP address or any ID, and it can't be linked to you. Google's sign-in script, loaded only when you connect Google Tasks, is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Removing access

You can remove MoveMyTodo's access at any time: Microsoft at account.live.com/consent/Manage (personal accounts) or myapps.microsoft.com (work or school), and Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
