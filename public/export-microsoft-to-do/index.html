How to export Microsoft To Do (free)

Microsoft To Do has no export button, and the export Microsoft promised before the June 30, 2027 shutdown isn't available yet (checked 26 November 2026). MoveMyTodo reads your lists through Microsoft's official API, read-only, and downloads them for free as CSV for Excel or Google Sheets, Markdown, JSON or a printable page, including steps, notes, due dates, reminders and repeat rules.

Scan my To Do lists

Last updated: November 26, 2026 · Free scan and backup · Read-only sign-in

Steps

  1. Go to MoveMyTodo and click Sign in with Microsoft.
  2. Allow read access to your tasks. Your lists load into the page.
  3. Untick lists you don't need and choose whether to include completed tasks.
  4. Click CSV for Excel / Sheets, Markdown, JSON or Printable page. For a PDF, open the printable page and print it to PDF.

What's in the export

Columns in the CSV
List, Title, StatusOne row per task, open or completed.
Due date, Reminder, RepeatsDue day, reminder time with your time zone, and the repeat rule in words (the JSON also has it as an iCalendar RRULE).
StepsAll steps in one cell, ticked or not, plus a “2/5 done” count.
Notes, Categories, LinksAs in To Do. Formatting is turned into plain text.
Created, CompletedDates for your own records.

File attachments aren't downloaded; the export marks which tasks have them so you can save those files from To Do.

When Microsoft's own export arrives

Microsoft says it will offer a way to export To Do data before June 30, 2027, but it hasn't said when, what format it will use or what it includes (checked 26 November 2026). It has also said an export doesn't mean another app can import it. When it ships, we'll test it and update this page with what it contains, and add a way to move that file to Google Tasks, Apple Reminders or Things 3. Until then, a backup you make today is the safe option.

Questions

How do I export Microsoft To Do?

Sign in to MoveMyTodo with your Microsoft account and download a free backup as CSV (opens in Excel and Google Sheets), Markdown, JSON or a printable page. Microsoft's own export isn't available yet as of 26 November 2026.

Is it safe to sign in? What can MoveMyTodo see?

MoveMyTodo asks Microsoft for read-only access to your tasks, so it cannot change or delete anything in To Do. Your tasks are read straight into your browser tab and are never sent to or stored on our servers. Close the tab and they're gone.

What if I wait until after June 30, 2027?

The apps will be gone, and Microsoft hasn't promised how long the export or API will keep working after that. Back up now; it takes a minute and costs nothing.

Does it work with a work or school account?

Often, but not always. Some organisations only let you sign in to apps an admin has approved; if you see an admin-approval message, ask your IT team or use the free backup another way. Personal Microsoft accounts work straight away.
