How to move Microsoft To Do to Apple Reminders

Apple Reminders has no import button, but a Mac can add reminders from a script. MoveMyTodo reads your To Do lists (read-only) and gives you an import file; one Terminal command adds a Reminders list for each To Do list, with notes, due dates, reminder times and flags, and iCloud syncs them to your iPhone and iPad.

On a Mac

  1. On MoveMyTodo, sign in with Microsoft and pick your lists.
  2. Open the Apple Reminders tab and click Download the Reminders import file. It saves import-to-reminders.js to Downloads.
  3. Open Terminal (⌘ Space, type “Terminal”) and run:
    osascript -l JavaScript ~/Downloads/import-to-reminders.js
  4. Allow Terminal to control Reminders when macOS asks. When it prints “Done: added … reminders”, open Reminders.

The script only adds new lists. If you already have a Reminders list with the same name, the new one is called “… (To Do)”. Make sure iCloud is turned on for Reminders (System Settings → your name → iCloud) so the lists reach your iPhone.

What carries over

ImportantFlagged, with high priority.

RepeatsWritten into the notes; set them again in Reminders.
Completed tasksOptional; added and marked completed.

On an iPhone or iPad, without a Mac

There's a free route that uses Apple's own Exchange support, while To Do still works:

  1. On the iPhone, open Settings → Apps → Reminders → Reminders Accounts → Add Account (on older iOS: Settings → Mail → Accounts → Add Account) and choose Outlook.com. Sign in with your To Do account and turn on Reminders.
  2. Open Reminders. Your To Do lists appear under the Outlook account.
  3. Open each list, tap ⋯ → Select Reminders, select all, tap the folder icon and move them to an iCloud list.

Limits: To Do's steps, file attachments and some repeat rules don't come through this way, and anything left in the Outlook account disappears when To Do's service is switched off. Download the free backup first so you keep a full copy.

Questions

Can I move Microsoft To Do to Apple Reminders?

Yes, on a Mac: MoveMyTodo makes an import file that adds a Reminders list for each To Do list, with notes, due dates, reminder times and flags, and iCloud syncs it to your iPhone. Without a Mac, see the free iPhone method in our Apple Reminders guide.

What happens to repeating tasks and reminders?

Due dates move everywhere. Google Tasks, Apple Reminders and Things can't receive a repeat rule through an import, so MoveMyTodo writes it into the task's notes, for example “Repeats: Every month on day 1”, and you set it again in the new app.

Will moving delete anything from To Do?

No. MoveMyTodo only reads To Do, and in the new app it only adds new lists, never changing or deleting the lists you already have there.

Is MoveMyTodo free?

Scanning, the full backup (CSV, Markdown, JSON, printable) and Todoist files are free with no limits. Moving to Apple Reminders or Things is free for up to 25 tasks from one list; MoveMyTodo Pro moves everything, including to Google Tasks, for $6.99 once.
