MoveMyTodo is a small independent tool. Use it to back up and move your own tasks. Here are the few rules.

The service

MoveMyTodo reads your Microsoft To Do tasks with your permission and writes copies to files or to the app you choose. It only adds; it never deletes your tasks in To Do or in the destination app. Check the result in your new app before you stop using To Do. MoveMyTodo depends on Microsoft's and Google's services, which they can change or switch off.
