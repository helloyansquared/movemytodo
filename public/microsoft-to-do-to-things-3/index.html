How to move Microsoft To Do to Things 3

Things 3 has no Microsoft To Do importer. MoveMyTodo reads your To Do lists (read-only) and hands them to Things through Things' own link feature, so each list becomes a Things project with its tasks, steps as checklist items, notes, due dates as deadlines, reminder times and categories as tags. It works on the Mac, iPhone or iPad where Things is installed.

Steps

  1. On the device where Things is installed, open MoveMyTodo and click Sign in with Microsoft. Allow read access to your tasks.
  2. Untick any lists you don't want and download the free backup while you're there.
  3. In Move to your new app, choose Things 3 and click Make my Things buttons. You get one button per list (big lists are split into parts of 40 tasks).
  4. Tap the first button. Things opens and asks whether to enable Things URLs; choose Enable. The list arrives as a new project.
  5. Go back to the page and tap the next button. Wait for Things to finish each one before tapping the next.

If nothing happens when you tap a button, check the setting in Things: on a Mac, Things → Settings → General → Enable Things URLs; on iPhone or iPad, Settings → General → Things URLs.

What moves

Microsoft To Do → Things 3
ListsA new Things project for each list. Nothing already in Things is changed.
StepsChecklist items inside the to-do, ticked or not.
Due dates and remindersDue date becomes the deadline; a reminder becomes “When” with the same time.
Notes, links, categoriesNotes and links are kept; categories become tags; Important becomes an “Important” tag.
Completed tasksAdded as completed, so they go to the Logbook.
Repeating tasksThe rule is written into the notes (for example “Repeats: Every week on Thu”); set it again in Things.
File attachmentsNot moved. Save them from To Do first.

The other route: through Apple Reminders

Things can import from Apple Reminders (on a Mac: File → Import → Import From Reminders), so another way is To Do → Reminders → Things. It works, but it's two hops, and Things doesn't import lists that sit inside Reminders groups, so drag those out first. Things' own help page explains the import. Moving straight from To Do keeps steps as checklists, which the Reminders route loses.

Before you start

  • Things is a paid app for Mac, iPhone and iPad and syncs through its own Things Cloud. You need it installed on the device you use for the move.
  • You can try one list of up to 25 tasks for free. Moving all your lists is part of MoveMyTodo Pro.
  • To Do keeps your tasks until you stop using it, so you can compare both apps for a while.

Questions

Can I move Microsoft To Do to Things 3?

Yes. Things 3 has no Microsoft To Do importer, but MoveMyTodo turns each To Do list into a Things project through Things' own link feature: steps become checklist items, due dates become deadlines and categories become tags. Open MoveMyTodo on the Mac, iPhone or iPad where Things is installed and tap the buttons it makes.

What happens to repeating tasks and reminders?

Due dates move everywhere. Google Tasks, Apple Reminders and Things can't receive a repeat rule through an import, so MoveMyTodo writes it into the task's notes, for example “Repeats: Every month on day 1”, and you set it again in the new app.

Is it safe to sign in? What can MoveMyTodo see?

MoveMyTodo asks Microsoft for read-only access to your tasks, so it cannot change or delete anything in To Do. Your tasks are read straight into your browser tab and are never sent to or stored on our servers. Close the tab and they're gone.

Is MoveMyTodo free?

Scanning, the full backup (CSV, Markdown, JSON, printable) and Todoist files are free with no limits. Moving to Apple Reminders or Things is free for up to 25 tasks from one list; MoveMyTodo Pro moves everything, including to Google Tasks, for $6.99 once. (Google Tasks is Pro-only while Google verifies MoveMyTodo, because Google caps unverified apps at 100 users.)
