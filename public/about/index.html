About MoveMyTodo

MoveMyTodo is a small browser tool made by Yan, an independent developer at YanSquared, for people leaving Microsoft To Do before it retires on June 30, 2027. It isn't made by or affiliated with Microsoft, Google, Apple, Cultured Code (Things) or Doist (Todoist).

Published November 26, 2026 · Last updated: November 26, 2026

Why it exists

When Microsoft announced on November 18, 2026 that the To Do apps would retire, people with years of lists had nowhere obvious to take them. Todoist and TickTick have their own importers, but Google Tasks, Apple Reminders and Things 3 don't. MoveMyTodo fills that gap, and gives everyone a free backup they control, whichever app they pick.

How it handles your data

  • Sign-in is read-only: MoveMyTodo can't change or delete anything in To Do.
  • Your tasks are read straight into your browser tab and written directly to the app you choose. They never reach our servers, and nothing is kept after you close the tab.
  • No cookies, analytics or ads. The full privacy policy explains each permission.
  • The code is open source, so anyone can check what it does: github.com/helloyansquared/movemytodo.

How the facts are checked

Dates and rules about the retirement come from Microsoft's own support page, “Moving from Microsoft To Do to Microsoft Planner”, and Microsoft's announcement. Every guide shows when it was last checked, and the shutdown page keeps a dated log of changes. When Microsoft updates its guidance or ships its export tool, the guides are updated.

Pricing

Scanning and the full backup are free with no limits. Moving up to 25 tasks from one list is free; MoveMyTodo Pro moves everything for $6.99 once, with a 14-day refund. Google Tasks is part of MoveMyTodo Pro while Google reviews the app, because Google limits unverified apps to 100 users.
